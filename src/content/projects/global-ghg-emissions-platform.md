## Overview

Delivering auditable, customer-level carbon footprint visibility across a decentralized global contract logistics network requires unifying heterogeneous utility, facility, and transport data feeds.

I architected and deployed the **Global Facility-Level GHG Emissions Reporting Platform** alongside an MCL-wide transportation emissions visibility program.

## Implementation

- **Facility Emissions Pipeline:** Combined Python ingestion pipelines, DataLoader workflows, and semantic Power BI models across **125+ sites** in all five Maersk Contract Logistics regions.
- **Transport Data Lake Integration:** Routed country-level Transportation Management System (TMS) feeds into a centralized data lake to enable cross-regional carbon allocation and reporting.
- **Strategic Alignment:** Provided customer-level emissions transparency supporting Maersk's broader energy transition and enterprise sustainability commitments.
