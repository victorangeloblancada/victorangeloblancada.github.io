## Overview

Allocating weekly advertising spend across television, digital, print, and seasonal campaigns across multiple Asia-Pacific markets involves non-linear diminishing returns (ad stock and saturation curves) and tight cross-portfolio constraints.

While owning the APAC data technology framework and leading IPG Mediabrands' global predictive modeling hub, I engineered a custom optimization and econometric modeling suite serving Fortune 500 brands including **Johnson & Johnson** and **Coca-Cola**.

## Key Systems Built

- **Cross-Market Portfolio Optimizer:** Built the first tool in the region capable of joint multi-year, cross-market, cross-portfolio weekly media optimization, generating client sales lifts of **up to 13%**.
- **Bayesian MCMC Marketing Mix Models:** Quantified marginal ROI and carryover effects per channel to guide annual C-suite budget allocations.
- **Automated Predictive Flu-Tracker:** Combined weather telemetry and search interest signals to forecast seasonal flu surges and schedule cold/flu medicine campaigns across APAC.
