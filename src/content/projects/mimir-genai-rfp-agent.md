## Overview

Global logistics requests for proposals (RFPs) require synthesizing operational specifications, historical pricing assumptions, compliance rules, and regional solution engineering playbooks under tight commercial deadlines.

At Maersk Contract Logistics (MCL), I designed and deployed **Mimir**, a production Generative AI agent built to assist solution design teams across five global regions.

## Architecture & Capabilities

- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) & Tool Calling:** Connects language model reasoning directly to curated internal knowledge bases and operational calculators rather than relying on ungrounded parametric memory.
- **Global Pilot & Rollout:** Piloted with regional solution design leaders worldwide, reducing initial RFP review and drafting cycles from days to hours.
- **Best-Practice Playbook Recommendations (Phase 2):** Extends Mimir from reactive question-answering into proactive solution recommendations drawn from MCL operational playbooks.
- **Knowledge Base Hygiene (Loopio Pipeline):** Paired with an NLP deduplication and answer-synthesis pipeline consolidating **1,524** historical RFP knowledge entries to improve retrieval precision and reduce subject-matter-expert review overhead.
