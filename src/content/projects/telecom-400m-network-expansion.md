## Overview

Expanding last-mile fiber infrastructure is capital-intensive and irreversible: building into low-yield corridors strands capital, while under-building cedes enterprise market share.

Deployed on-site in North America with Mitchell Madison Group, I led the data science workstream directing a **$400MM USD** fiber network expansion for a major telecommunications carrier.

## Quantitative Approach

- **Terabyte-Scale Graph & Relational Modeling:** Integrated commercial parcel, prospect density, and existing network topology across **SQL Server** and **Neo4j**.
- **Bottom-Up Customer Lifetime Value Simulation:** Forecasted expected revenue, churn risk, and acquisition probabilities per prospect location rather than relying on coarse ZIP-level averages.
- **Constrained Graph Optimization:** Solved for the high-ROI expansion topology under the $400MM capital ceiling and presented recommendations directly to senior C-level executives.
