## Overview

Large fulfillment centers operate as tightly coupled queueing and routing networks where slotting, wave release, and labor allocation interact non-linearly.

I co-led the **Digital Twin & Warehouse AI** initiative at Maersk Contract Logistics' **1.2 million square foot** Groveport distribution center—a first-of-kind deployment across MCL.

## System Components

- **Spatial Ground Truth via 3D Photogrammetry:** Captured physical aisle geometry, rack configurations, and travel paths to eliminate layout drift between CAD plans and floor reality.
- **Discrete-Event Simulation (`AnyLogic` & `FlexSim`):** Modeled inbound receiving, putaway, picking waves, and outbound staging under shifting SKU velocity and order profiles.
- **ML-Based Wave & Task Optimization:** Applied machine learning heuristics to balance picker travel distance, congestion, and SLA completion times.
- **Operational Telemetry (`AWS QuickSight`):** Exposed scenario comparisons and floor performance metrics to site and regional operations leadership.
